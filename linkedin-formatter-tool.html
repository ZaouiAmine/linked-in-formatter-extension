How this actually works. Select the word "growth" and click B: the plain letters growth (U+0067 U+0072…) become the Mathematical Bold Unicode letters 𝗴𝗿𝗼𝘄𝘁𝗵 (U+1D5F4…) — a different, pre‑bolded character for every letter. LinkedIn's editor just displays whatever characters you paste, so it renders as bold text with no formatting markup involved. This is also why bold Unicode text still looks bold in a text message, a tweet, or a plain‑text email — and why it can't be spell‑checked or read aloud correctly by screen readers, since assistive tech sees "𝗴𝗿𝗼𝘄𝘁𝗵" as unrelated symbols, not the word "growth".
Formatting is applied live in your browser — nothing you type here is sent anywhere.
